Full datasets (harness/dexor_full.cpp, server; no timing): full_upstream.csv.
Bits/value include the 8-byte count per 1,000-value block.
13/16 datasets decode within 2.3e-16. Values above 1e-9 relative error:
  LAT 135,738 / 24.9M (max 0.32)   AP 5,648 / 137.7M (max 0.0101)
  LON 3,130 / 24.9M (max 0.01)
Causes are upstream behaviours, kept by policy (they do not prevent meaningful
aggregate metrics) and reported as validation warnings:
  1. decoder applies the prefix's sign to |beta| (DoubleDeXORDecoder.java:109,
     DoubleDeXOREncoder.java:120): values just below a carry of the shared
     prefix are reconstructed on the wrong side (39999.997 -> 40000.003);
  2. the tail search's 1e-6 integer test (39999.997 -> 40000), and
  3. encoder/decoder previous-value drift after an inexact value.
A one-condition sign guard was evaluated: LON 0, LAT 3,037 (max 0.025) values
above 1e-9, +0.02% size, no change on sampled blocks. Not shipped (policy).
The previous exact port had 0 mismatches but 11.6-36.2 bits/value
(new: 6.6-30.7). Per-dataset sizes: table.csv.

Deviations from upstream (only where upstream crashes or emits undecodable
output): power-of-ten index out of range -> exception encoding; q outside the
5-bit field [-20,11] -> exception encoding; decoder always resets the
contraction counter on overflow (encoder does; bitstream unchanged); 8-byte
count framing and malformed-stream checks.

Harness paths reflect the audit workspace; datasets and jars are not included.
